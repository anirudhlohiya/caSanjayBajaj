FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
The official app of S N Bajaj And Co, Chartered Accountants, Surat, for our clients.

NOT A GOVERNMENT APP: S N Bajaj And Co is a private firm of Chartered Accountants. This app is not a government app and does not represent, and is not affiliated with, any government entity.

Send your documents, follow your filings and stay on top of due dates, all in one place, without chasing emails or WhatsApp threads.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Upload GST and ITR documents month by month: bills, bank statements, registers and more, straight from your phone
• See what is needed for each period and how much you have already sent
• Track the status of your GST returns and income tax filings
• Download your filed returns, reports and certificates whenever you need them
• Get reminders before due dates so nothing is missed
• Receive notifications when your CA files a return, needs a document or replies to you
• Raise a support ticket or talk to an expert at our office
• Use the app in English, Hindi or Gujarati

SECURE BY DESIGN
• Your documents are stored privately and opened only through short-lived secure links
• All data is encrypted in transit
• Your data is never sold or used for advertising
• You can delete your account at any time from your Profile

WHO IT IS FOR
Clients of S N Bajaj And Co who want their GST and income tax work handled from their phone.

OFFICIAL SOURCES
The due dates and tax rules shown in the app come from these official government sources. Always check them for the latest notifications:
• GST Portal: https://www.gst.gov.in
• CBIC (GST notifications): https://cbic-gst.gov.in
• Income Tax e-Filing: https://www.incometax.gov.in
• Income Tax Department (TDS / TCS): https://incometaxindia.gov.in
